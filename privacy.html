Privacy Policy — Under Center: QB Career Sim

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Under Center is a single-player game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

  • No account, no sign-in. You never give us a name, email or phone number.
  • Local saves only. Your careers (including the character name you type) are stored on your own device and are never uploaded.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app has no third-party SDKs and does not use the advertising identifier.
  • Game Center. If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, achievements and leaderboard scores are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your personal information.
  • Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Photos. If you choose "Save image" in the career book, the app asks permission to add the image to your Photos library. It can only add images and cannot read your library.
  • Children. The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Under Center is a work of fiction. All teams, leagues, awards and people are made up. Any resemblance to real ones is coincidental.
